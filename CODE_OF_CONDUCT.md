# Community code of conduct

Participate respectfully. Welcome different experience levels, give actionable feedback, and discuss technical decisions on their merits. Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing, and publication of private information are unacceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation, or ban repeat or severe offenders. Enforcement should consider severity and context and should avoid exposing reporter identities. Maintainers are expected to follow the same standards.

A dedicated private conduct contact has not yet been established. Do not post private incident details publicly; ask the maintainer to establish a private channel. Contact setup is an outstanding community-launch requirement.

This is a project-specific initial policy, not a claim of adoption of an external code-of-conduct framework.
